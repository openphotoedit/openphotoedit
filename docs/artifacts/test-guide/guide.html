01

Why this is worth building为什么值得做

Photoshop users pay for AI that is metered. The single-app plan is $22.99 a month with 25 generative credits, about two premium generations. The complaints are specific: "Nothing it generates is even worth keeping, yet credits are deducted." Cancelling costs a fee Adobe paid a $150M settlement over in March 2026.

Photoshop 用户在为按次计费的 AI 付钱。单应用方案每月 22.99 美元，只含 25 个生成点数，大约够用两次高级生成。用户的抱怨很具体：“生成的东西根本没法用，点数却照扣。”取消订阅还要付违约金，Adobe 为此在 2026 年 3 月支付了 1.5 亿美元的和解金。

The free alternatives each give something up. Photopea carries ads, is closed source and charges credits for AI. Affinity is free but needs a Canva login, and all its AI is Canva Premium. GIMP has no web version, drops PSD layer styles and adjustment layers, and ships no AI.

免费替代品各有取舍。Photopea 有广告、不开源，AI 功能要点数。Affinity 免费但必须登录 Canva，所有 AI 功能都要 Canva 高级版。GIMP 没有网页版，打开 PSD 会丢掉图层样式和调整图层，也没有 AI。

The structural gap is the meter. A product that runs AI in its own cloud has to charge for it. Removing objects, selecting subjects, cutting out backgrounds, upscaling, denoising and culling all run on the user's own hardware here, at no cost per photo, so they can stay unlimited.

结构性的空档在于“计费”。在自己云端跑 AI 的产品必须收费。而在这里，去除物体、选择主体、抠图、放大、降噪和挑片都在用户自己的设备上运行，每张照片没有成本，所以可以一直不限次数。

What a Rust core buys: one engine compiled to WebAssembly for the browser and natively for a local desktop server, with PSD round-trip as the hard part a weekend project cannot reach. No open-source editor today combines faithful PSD round-trip with local AI in a browser tab.

Rust 内核带来的好处：同一个引擎既编译成 WebAssembly 在浏览器里运行，也能原生编译成本地桌面服务器；而 PSD 的完整读写，正是周末项目做不到的难点。目前没有任何开源编辑器能在浏览器标签页里同时做到忠实的 PSD 读写和本地 AI。

What real files found that the unit tests did not真实文件发现、而单元测试没发现的问题

Unit tests use small synthetic inputs. These came from opening real photos, a real Photoshop file and real camera raw files. Open items are listed first.

单元测试用的是很小的合成数据。下面这些问题来自打开真实照片、真实的 Photoshop 文件和真实的相机 RAW 文件。未解决的问题排在前面。

Open未解决

Layer styles in a real PSD do not match Photoshop真实 PSD 中的图层样式与 Photoshop 不一致

In layer_effects.psd, Outer Glow draws as a dark smudge instead of a white glow, Bevel & Emboss comes out flat, Inner Glow and Drop Shadow are too faint, and Pattern Overlay is not supported (the app says so). Stroke, Colour Overlay, Gradient Overlay and Satin match. Side by side in figure 20.

在 layer_effects.psd 中，外发光被画成一团暗色污渍而不是白色光晕，斜面和浮雕是平的，内发光和投影太淡，图案叠加不支持（应用会提示）。描边、颜色叠加、渐变叠加和光泽与 Photoshop 一致。对比见图 20。

Open未解决

Fast removal leaves a ghost of a walking person快速去除会留下行人的残影

On the street photo, Remove in Fast mode (MI-GAN, 1.9 s) leaves a visible smear where the person was. Best mode (LaMa, 11.5 s) is much cleaner but still leaves a faint trace of the legs. Small objects such as a lapel pin disappear cleanly in both. Figure 24.

在街景照片上，“快速”模式的去除（MI-GAN，1.9 秒）会在原来行人的位置留下明显涂抹痕迹。“最佳”模式（LaMa，11.5 秒）干净得多，但仍能隐约看到腿部轮廓。像西装领针这样的小物体，两种模式都能干净去除。见图 24。

Open未解决

Raw files develop flatter than the camera's own JPEGRAW 显影比相机自带 JPEG 更平淡

Across Canon, Fujifilm, Nikon and Sony files the colours are natural, with no zipper artefacts or magenta highlights, but contrast and saturation sit below the camera's embedded preview. The default tone curve needs a stronger "camera look". Figure 21.

佳能、富士、尼康和索尼的文件颜色自然，没有拉链状伪影，高光也没有偏品红，但对比度和饱和度都低于相机内嵌的预览图。默认色调曲线需要更接近“机内直出”的效果。见图 21。

Open未解决

PSD composites still differ on 81 of 244 files244 个 PSD 中仍有 81 个合成结果有差异

Across the 269-file corpus the layer tree matches psd-tools on every file and every export re-imports, but our flattened image is within an average error of 3 (out of 255) on 163 of 244 files. The causes are knockout groups, layers clipped to pass-through groups, Hue/Saturation range maths, Vivid Light, Hard Mix and layer styles.

在 269 个文件的测试集中，每个文件的图层结构都与 psd-tools 一致，每次导出都能重新导入；但在 244 个可对比的文件里，只有 163 个的合成图平均误差在 3 以内（满值 255）。原因包括挖空组、剪贴到“穿透”组的图层、色相/饱和度按色域调整的算法、亮光、实色混合以及图层样式。

Open未解决

AI denoise is slow on the CPU pathAI 降噪在 CPU 路径上很慢

Headless Chromium has no GPU, so AI ran on the CPU: denoising a 900 × 899 image took 15.8 s and the detailed 4× upscale of a 268 × 276 crop took 29 s. The GPU path is expected to be several times faster but was not measured in this pass.

无头 Chromium 没有 GPU，所以 AI 都在 CPU 上运行：一张 900 × 899 的图降噪用了 15.8 秒，268 × 276 的小图做“细节”4 倍放大用了 29 秒。GPU 路径预计快好几倍，但本次没有测量。

Fixed已修复

Content-aware scale tore the red curtains内容识别缩放撕裂了红色窗帘

With skin protection on, the portrait's red curtains passed the skin-colour test and were protected instead of the background, so seams cut through them. The colour rule was tightened and stray matches outside solid areas are now ignored; the face is preserved and the curtains are clean.

开启“保护肤色”后，人像照片里的红色窗帘通过了肤色检测，被当成需要保护的区域，结果接缝从窗帘中间穿过。现在收紧了颜色规则，并忽略实心区域之外的零星匹配；人脸完整保留，窗帘也干净了。

Fixed已修复

Content-aware fill left seams, then smeared skin into the suit内容识别填充先留下接缝，之后又把皮肤抹进西装

A 200 × 200 hole on the curtain first showed faint seams; the first fix pulled skin texture into a hole on the suit. Both were caught by looking at the output. The fill now scores an error of 2.1 on the curtain against 24.5 for a plain colour fill.

窗帘上 200 × 200 的空洞一开始留下了淡淡的接缝；第一次修复又把皮肤纹理填进了西装上的空洞。这两个问题都是看输出图片发现的。现在窗帘上的填充误差为 2.1，而纯色填充是 24.5。

Fixed已修复

Editing text wrote hidden steps into history编辑文字时往历史记录里写入了隐藏步骤

Text editing and transform previews hid the original layer with an undoable "Hide Layer" step, and Lite's look previews left hidden redo entries. Both now hide layers in the viewport only, and previews render on a throwaway copy of the document.

编辑文字和变换预览时，原图层是通过一个可撤销的“隐藏图层”步骤藏起来的；简易版生成风格预览时也会留下隐藏的重做记录。现在两者都只在视图里隐藏图层，预览则在文档的临时副本上渲染。

Fixed已修复

Three tool bugs caught in screenshots截图里发现的三个工具问题

Rotated crops left empty corners; Pixelate stamped every visible layer and covered the arrows and text; tool commands sent reactive state objects that could not cross into the engine's worker. Quick select also queued a backlog while dragging (145–195 ms per step); strokes now merge while a step is running.

旋转裁剪会留下空白角落；马赛克会把所有可见图层合并，盖住箭头和文字；工具发出的命令带着响应式状态对象，无法传给引擎的工作线程。快速选择在拖动时还会排起长队（每步 145–195 毫秒），现在执行中的步骤会合并后续笔画。

The bundle names Hugging Face and GitHub, but never calls them代码包里出现了 Hugging Face 和 GitHub 地址，但从不访问

The shipped worker contains 35 upstream model URLs. They are provenance labels for the model licence screen; downloads go through the app's own origin, and the network test recorded zero requests leaving localhost while both profiles were used.

发布的工作线程代码里有 35 个模型上游地址，它们只是模型许可说明里的来源标注；实际下载都走应用自己的域名。网络测试在使用两种模式的整个过程中，记录到离开 localhost 的请求为零。

Known limits已知限制

  • Working name, not deployed. “Photoshop” is Adobe's trademark, so nothing is published under this name. Everything runs locally; there is no public URL, account or store build yet.暂定名称，尚未上线。“Photoshop”是 Adobe 的注册商标，因此不会以此名称发布。目前一切都在本地运行，没有公开网址、账号或应用商店版本。
  • AI was tested on the CPU only. Headless browsers have no GPU; the WebGPU path and its canary were not measured in this pass. Generative fill and other native-GPU features are not built.AI 只在 CPU 上测试过。无头浏览器没有 GPU，本次没有测量 WebGPU 路径及其一致性检查。生成式填充等需要本地 GPU 的功能尚未开发。
  • 8-bit documents. 16- and 32-bit PSD, TIFF and raw files are converted to 8-bit on open.文档为 8 位。16 位和 32 位的 PSD、TIFF 与 RAW 文件打开时会转换成 8 位。
  • PSDs we write are not verified in Photoshop itself. Adjustment, text and smart-object layers reopen in our reader and in psd-tools; nobody has reopened them in Photoshop.我们写出的 PSD 尚未在 Photoshop 里验证。调整、文字和智能对象图层能被我们自己的读取器和 psd-tools 重新打开，但还没人用 Photoshop 打开过。
  • The photo library is not wired in. Folder browsing, assisted culling, batch export and Actions exist in code but are not reachable from either profile.照片库尚未接入界面。文件夹浏览、辅助挑片、批量导出和动作已写好代码，但两种模式里都还无法使用。
  • English only. The eight-language runtime is in place; the seven translations are not written.目前只有英文。八种语言的翻译框架已就位，但其余七种语言的译文还没写。
  • Phones were emulated, not real. iPhone sizing and touch ran in Chromium; no real device or simulator was used. onnxruntime-web cannot run on iOS 17 Safari.手机为模拟测试。iPhone 尺寸和触摸是在 Chromium 中模拟的，没有使用真机或模拟器。onnxruntime-web 无法在 iOS 17 的 Safari 上运行。
  • Some operations are slow in the browser. Surface blur takes 8 s on a 12 MP image in wasm, quick select 145–195 ms per brush step, AI denoise 15.8 s for 900 × 899 on CPU.部分操作在浏览器中较慢。1200 万像素图片的表面模糊在 wasm 中需要 8 秒，快速选择每个笔画步骤 145–195 毫秒，CPU 上对 900 × 899 的图做 AI 降噪需要 15.8 秒。
  • Single timing runs. Browser times are one run each on a busy machine; treat them as orders of magnitude, not benchmarks.计时只跑了一次。浏览器中的时间是在负载较高的机器上各跑一次得到的，只能看数量级，不能当作基准。
